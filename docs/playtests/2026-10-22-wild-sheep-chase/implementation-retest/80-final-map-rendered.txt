Aspen Desktop Continuity Verification L4, clattering hooves and surprised yelps cut through the afternoon. Frantic bleating follows, close enough to draw your attention toward a fluffy white sheep bounding toward your group.

It has a black face and curled horns. An ornate scroll is held in its mouth, and as the sheep comes near, it waves the scroll toward you.

The sheep is right here, the scroll plainly visible between its jaws. What do you do?

Source references
You:
I read the scroll aloud and listen to the sheep.
DM

As you read the ornate scroll aloud, the sheep’s frantic bleating shifts into cultured Common, touched by an unmistakable bleat. “I am Finethir Shinebright, a wizard in dire need of aid.”

He explains that he needs help taking an extremely powerful magical artifact from a dangerous, possibly insane wizard. The scroll hangs from his mouth as he speaks; his curled horns frame an intent, woolly face. The sounds of hooves and startled yelps still carry from nearby.

Finethir has made his plea. What do you say or do?

NPC · Finethir Shinebright
You:
Finethir, where can I find Noke and the wand, and how do I get there?
DM

Finethir answers through the scroll, his voice still carrying that odd bleat. “Noke lives in my former home, southeast of town. Take the road out of town until a well-trodden path branches off, a few miles out. Follow it through sparse woodland for about a mile, and you’ll reach the house.”

He adds that Noke keeps the wand on him and leaves the home only if absolutely necessary. The ornate scroll remains between the sheep’s jaws as the sounds of hooves and startled yelps linger nearby. What do you do next?

Source references
You:
I stand beside Finethir in plain view and wait for the approaching pursuers to arrive.
DM

You remain beside Finethir as the commotion reaches you. A hulking half-orc pushes through the crowd, one hand resting on his greatsword. Three large wolves pace with him, iron collars around their necks; a huge cloaked figure follows, its heavy footfalls audible above the uproar.

The half-orc fixes his small eyes on the sheep. “That sheep is Master Noke’s... he desires to have it back.” Finethir stays close at your side, the ornate scroll still in his mouth. The pursuers have arrived, and the half-orc’s demand hangs between you. What do you do?

You:
I stop hiding. I hand Finethir over to Guz and stand aside, then openly walk beside them as they leave. I ask Guz to take me to Noke.
DM

You stop hiding and place Finethir in Guz’s custody. The half-orc takes the sheep with him; the wolves pace alongside as the group sets off toward Noke’s home. You walk openly beside them, and Guz agrees to take you there.

The journey brings you to the approach to Noke’s home. Finethir remains with Guz, while the way ahead leads toward the place he described—the wizard’s former home, where Noke keeps the wand. What do you do as you reach it?

Source references
You:
I stop hiding and openly follow Guz to Noke’s home. I want to speak to Noke, and I walk along the route they take.
DM

You stop hiding and follow the route openly. The road gives way to a narrow, well-trodden track through a gap in the bushes, winding among tall oaks. Many overlapping prints mark the earth. Beyond them, the woodland is sparse and quiet.

The track continues ahead through the trees, but Noke’s home is not yet in view. What do you do?

Source references
You:
I continue along the well-trodden track through the tall oaks until I reach Noke’s home.
DM

Continuing along the track, you come within sight of a strange house woven through four sturdy oaks. Thick platforms of living branches span the trees; above the ground, a bowl-shaped garden is filled with flowers, and an enclosed structure of wood and branches rises beyond it.

On the lawn, three great apes lounge or play with oversized dice beside iron greatswords. Two small huts stand beneath the platforms, with a large outhouse nearby. You’re still outside, with the house ahead—how do you approach?

Source references
You:
I stay in plain view and call out, "Noke! I have come to talk about Finethir and your wand. Please show yourself." I wait for an answer.
DM

Noke does not come out to meet you, but from outside you can see the three great apes lounging or playing with their oversized dice beside the house. Above them, the flowered garden and woven platforms rise through the four oaks. Noke is working inside the central platform.

The apes remain on the lawn, their iron greatswords nearby. The huts and large outhouse stand beneath the platforms; the house itself is still ahead of you. You can call to Noke from here or choose how to approach the structure.
