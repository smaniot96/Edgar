# Whole campaign spine — v2.0
Input: source_kind; original AI request OR full-page OCR evidence/partition readings; verified creature catalogue. Output: Spine.
Establish premise, setting, opening hook, broad ending, chapters, major places/recurring characters, global truths and future reveals before chapter detail. For AI create directly from premise/theme/size; small means a compact complete adventure (usually 1–3 chapters), not a bare outline. For PDF cover the entire supplied adventure, with source chapters and physical page inventories; keep appendix/map evidence attached to useful chapters. Source order, play_order and chronology are distinct.
Use unique concise stable lowercase IDs (e.g. ch_lantern, npc_keeper, place_mill, secret_bell). Chapter briefs identify the dramatic purpose, practical situation and connections. Every secret has known_by, setup/reveal chapters and a reveal condition based on earned discovery. Name relevant entity_ids, secret_ids and genuine chapter dependencies. The public hook never discloses private causes. Include useful DM guidance and an achievable ending that follows meaningful player choices. Do not specify a hero. Keep the registry compact; chapter-local additions can be authored later.
For PDF, use important_visual_pages to request targeted interpretation of meaningful map connectivity, table relationships or ambiguous stat layouts that OCR alone cannot establish. Name only supplied physical pages; decorative art needs no interpretation. Chapters will receive the resulting visual evidence. For AI return an empty list.
