# Required engine capability review — v1.0

Input: every exact compiled capability gap, the complete authored record registry, campaign goals/ending, executable objects/checks/mechanics and topology. Output: CompatibilityReview with exactly one decision per supplied capability_id. Assess the prepared adventure, whether PDF based or AI generated. The PDF's actual procedures and outcomes remain ground truth for source-based play; no source fidelity claim follows from a schema-valid adaptation.

For each gap determine whether an essential goal, encounter identity, consequential choice or finale outcome depends on it. Optional unsupported detail may be optional only when the adventure remains coherent, challenging and completable without that operation; explain the concrete available play. A spell list gap is not automatically fatal if the actor has an effective supported combat identity and meaningful alternatives. One executable spell does not establish support for the rest of its spell list or riders.

Use supported_adaptation ONLY when the existing authored records explicitly establish a supported alternative that achieves the necessary outcome, with alternative_quote copied exactly from one of record_ids. A missing procedure is not repaired by saying a DM can improvise it. Retaining prose, rolling a check, narrating success, taking custody of an item, or writing an adaptation note does not execute transformation, charges, healing, damage, conditions or persistent effects. Do not invent an alternative during review. A finale whose goal is restoration is not completed by merely recovering its wand. A material source change must be explicit and cannot be called source-faithful.

Use blocked when a necessary operation lacks supported execution and no existing legitimate alternative achieves its outcome. Explain the consequential gap precisely and identify only authored records that need correction or consume the required fact. A blocked decision becomes a material finding and holds publication until repaired or engine support is provided; do not suppress it to make an adventure publishable. Use optional for ordinary dialogue/travel/discovery/custody or fixed-DC checks when their authored outcomes are already supported independently and the particular declared gap is immaterial. Never claim an unsupported effect or private reference is committed state.

required_for_completion must agree with the actual authored goals and ending. evidence must cite concrete authored requirements and available compiled operations. record_ids must exist in the supplied records. alternative_quote is empty unless supported_adaptation. Review every gap once, without collapsing unrelated item, check or actor limitations.
