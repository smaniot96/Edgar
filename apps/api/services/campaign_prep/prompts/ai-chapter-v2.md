# AI adventure chapter — v1.0
Input: the accepted premise, cast and full outline, requested_chapter (its plan, ID and position), story_so_far (accepted earlier chapters: their summaries, goals, events and new identities), existing_ids, verified_creatures (exact names with challenge ratings), size, size_profile and preferences. Output: one AiChapterDraft for requested_chapter only. Later chapters are written after you and receive your continuity_summary.

Write size_profile.events_per_chapter events and size_profile.goals_per_chapter goals. An event is a meaningful situation at a place, not a scripted action: give it a trigger, timing, a player-safe public_description of what is perceived on arrival, and dm_notes with motives, opportunities, clues, tactics, alternative approaches and consequences. Mix exploration, social interaction, discovery and, where it serves the story, combat, as the chapter's archetype suggests. Connect events with next_event_ids inside this chapter; allow non-linear play where it fits.

Goals describe outcomes the heroes can achieve by any credible creative approach. Mark required only what the adventure truly cannot continue without; optional goals reward exploration. Every event serves at least one goal and every goal lists its events.

Continuity: reference shared cast entities and secrets by their existing IDs, and earlier chapters' identities from story_so_far. Never redeclare an existing ID; new_entities and new_secrets are only for genuinely new local people, places, factions, clues and private truths, with new IDs not in existing_ids (prefixing them with the chapter's slug keeps them distinct). Every event location_id is a place. The heroes arrive through the leads from the previous chapter; acknowledge how. Follow the plan: at least one event at primary_location_id; reference every secret in sets_up_secret_ids in an event's secret_ids and foreshadow it in dm_notes without disclosing it; reference every secret in reveals_secret_ids in an event's secret_ids and make it discoverable through a reveal or dm_notes with an earned condition. Keep consequential truths out of public descriptions until earned. Show the antagonist_pressure in the world.

Reveals expose a hidden entity (a clue, a person's true nature, a hidden place) with earned public_text and a private condition; reveal entity_ids are entities present in the event. Encounters name an NPC entity as actor_id, an exact creature name from verified_creatures that suits the intended levels and the fiction, a quantity and tactics. Use encounters only where combat has a role; offer ways to avoid or end fights. Checks are optional fixed-DC skill, ability or tool checks (for example insight, athletics, arcana, thieves_tools), never the only solution. items give important objects (represented by clue entities) clear operating procedures.

running_notes is the chapter's practical running reference: geography, NPC voices and attitudes, timing, rewards and what happens if the heroes delay. continuity_summary tells later chapter authors what this chapter establishes, its possible outcomes and its open threads.

When revision is supplied, return the complete corrected chapter that fixes every listed problem and keeps sound content and IDs unchanged.
