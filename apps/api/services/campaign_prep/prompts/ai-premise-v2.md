# AI adventure premise — v1.0
Input: the owner's request (optional title hint and theme), size, size_profile, preferences and rules edition. Output: one AiPremise. This is the first of several sequential calls; the cast, chapter outline and each chapter are written later from your premise.

Start from the antagonist, as published adventures do. Give a named antagonist a believable motive, methods and a weakness the heroes can discover and use. Write the antagonist's scheme as a timeline of what happens if nobody interferes: at least two steps, each with its consequence. The adventure is what the heroes do about that scheme.

Write a player-safe hook that gives a compelling reason to act at once, a central question the adventure answers, and real stakes. The ending lists credible outcomes that depend on the heroes' choices, including partial success; never a single scripted finish. Scale ambition to size_profile.arc: a small adventure is one tight situation, a medium one needs room for a hub or investigation, a site and a climax.

Honour the theme and preferences when supplied; otherwise choose a fresh, specific premise with a concrete place, a distinctive tone and at least one memorable twist the outline can reveal later. Avoid generic chosen-one framing. level_recommendation describes the intended level range for the size. dm_guidance gives the DM concise advice for running this adventure's tone and pacing.

When revision is supplied, return a complete corrected premise that fixes every listed problem and keeps what was sound.
