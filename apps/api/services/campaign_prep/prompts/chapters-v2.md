# One coherent chapter with its original evidence — v2.4
Input: requested_chapter (ONE chapter), compact campaign dossier, all_chapter_briefs, shared_entities, shared_truths including future reveals, accepted_dependencies, full relevant source_evidence with physical page numbers, optional original page images, verified_creatures index plus relevant_source_profiles, original AI request and preferences. Output: Bundles containing EXACTLY ONE chapter whose chapter_id matches requested_chapter.id.

Author goals, events, NPCs/motives/knowledge, clues, places, secrets, timing and encounters together in this single call. Reuse shared IDs; new_entities/new_secrets are additions only, not duplicate shared entries. Prefix local IDs with the chapter ID. Keep new identities consistent with the future chapter briefs and accepted dependency commitments. Every goal references useful events and every event its goals. next_event_ids connect within this bundle. Distinct events represent meaningful situations, not single scripted actions.

Prepare an enjoyable adventure based on the PDF or AI request. Its own plot, motives, clues and consequences must make sense. You may combine, omit, invent or adapt details, encounters and timing; reproducing every original detail is not a requirement. Ordinary harmless differences need no change log. Briefly establish material continuity or geography changes so the DM has one consistent version to run. Original pages support compatible missing detail rather than silently overriding the accepted adaptation.

map_context identifies selected original chapter images by source_page reference, physical page and source/image hashes. Some selected images contain tables or profiles rather than maps; inspect the actual pixels. When using original geography, check directions, doors, stairs, access, heights and room connections against the actual map and keyed prose, never OCR reading order, numeric room order or a previous summary. A coherent geography adaptation is acceptable when all prepared guidance agrees on it. Preserve source uncertainty instead of claiming an unseen connection. Do not add an exhaustive route guide when focused running notes suffice.

Set source_pages to EXACTLY the requested chapter's pages (empty for AI); this is an evidence inventory, not an exhaustive reproduction promise. Read all supplied original pages, including shared appendices. source_notes supplies useful running detail absent from other records: characterization, motives, clue paths, tactics, conditional timing, spatial relationships, alternatives, rewards and consequences as needed by this adventure. Be specific where play depends on a quantity, condition or procedure, and keep that choice consistent across records. Do not repeat every event or transcribe the book. A page image can correct column ownership or OCR misreadings; do not merge adjacent stat blocks. Original OCR remains separately available, while the chapter must be useful on its own.

source_context_pages also includes supporting pages cited by this chapter's shared identities and truths. Use these original pages for relevant appendix procedures and profiles; their provenance may cite any supplied source_context_pages. Keep source_pages as the base chapter inventory. Give events and NPCs different IDs, register each encounter actor as a canonical NPC and participant, and connect next_event_ids only to actual events. A reveal's entity_id must identify what its public_text reveals and appear among the event's participants or clues; do not use an antagonist's ID for a different transformed guard.

Events are opportunities: public_description contains sensory facts perceived on arrival, with no unearned consequential secrets, future outcome or invented prior hero action. trigger, timing and dm_notes express this adventure's conditional behavior and allow alternate player solutions. Use concrete timing when it matters to running the scene. Do not make readily shared exposition artificially secret or demand a separate scripted action for every ordinary fact. visible_entity_ids contains visibly present entities with safe descriptions. Meaningful hidden discoveries use reveals with canonical entity_id, earned public_text and a private condition allowing credible approaches. Meeting an NPC does not automatically reveal their private plans or later truths.

Choose coherent encounter populations and distinct actor IDs for genuinely different creatures/groups or forms with different statistics. When the same individual moves between encounters, reuse its stable actor ID and exactly the same authoritative profile reference in every appearance. A disguise with unchanged statistics is the same individual and profile; preserve its conditional movement rather than adding another population. Define each mechanical profile once in this chapter's mechanical_profiles, before its events, with stable id, creature, complete source_stat_block and source_pages. Every encounter contains only actor_id, profile_id, quantity and per-appearance adaptation; inline statistics are forbidden by the output schema. Reuse known_mechanical_profiles by ID without copying their definitions. Multiple actors may share one profile; repeated rooms, appearances and disguises of one individual reuse both actor_id and profile_id. When using a printed profile, retain its original name and complete correct abilities, traits, actions and spell text/list with true provenance; the engine parses that text. A suitable supplied verified catalogue creature is also an acceptable adaptation: use its exact catalogue name and empty source_stat_block, and briefly identify a material substitution. A modified profile requires a supplied verified base and explicit documented changes. Never invent missing statistics from memory or claim an invented profile is verified. No encounter is necessary when combat has no useful role.

items records give important usable objects clear, consistent operating procedures, with a canonical clue entity_id and relevant event_ids. When relevant, explain eligibility, attunement, resource use, checks and consequences. The compiler supports object custody; advanced spell transformations/charge accounting are explicit engine limits, not implemented by these notes. checks records are independently executable ordinary fixed-DC skill/ability/tool checks appropriate to the prepared situation, with stable id, action, event_ids, engine check identifier, dc and coherent success/failure text. Do not turn escalating item-use checks or unimplemented spell effects into misleading static checks. Each check is a possible approach, never the only solution to a goal. Item descriptions must protect consequential secret procedures until discovered.

For AI, create rich playable detail directly from the request and shared plan; source_pages is empty and provenance authored. Do not import a previous campaign's design. Preserve player agency and treat the later-selected hero as protagonist.
